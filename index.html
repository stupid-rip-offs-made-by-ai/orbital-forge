      How to fly

      Building

      Stack parts top to bottom: command part → tanks → engine. A decoupler starts a new stage, so everything below it falls away when you stage. Engines only burn fuel from tanks in their own stage.

      Keep the first-stage TWR above ~1.3. Put a fin ring near the bottom so the rocket flies nose-first, and a parachute on top to get home.

      Getting to orbit

      1. Stage to light the engines and climb straight up.
      2. At ~1 km and ~100 m/s, slowly tilt east (right).
      3. By ~40 km be about halfway over. Cut the engines when your Apoapsis reaches ~80 km.
      4. Coast to Apoapsis (use time warp), face horizon/prograde and burn until Periapsis is above 70 km.

      To the moon

      From an 80 km orbit, wait (map view, time warp) until Lumen is rising about 110° ahead of you, then burn prograde until the orange trajectory shows a Lumen Pe. Burn retrograde at Lumen periapsis to capture, then land below 12 m/s with legs.
